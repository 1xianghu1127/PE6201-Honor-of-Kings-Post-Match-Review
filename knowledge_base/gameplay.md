# General gameplay and evidence rules

Source IDs: `official-hok-home`, `official-hok-guides`, `project-evidence-policy`

## Team objective

Honor of Kings is a 5v5 team game. The official site describes destroying the opposing crystal as the ultimate objective and identifies towers and jungle monsters as important battlefield elements. A post-match review should therefore avoid treating kills as the only measure of success.

## Raw totals versus team shares

Raw gold, damage dealt, and damage taken tend to grow with match duration. Team share is the primary comparison signal in this project, while raw totals and duration are supporting evidence. A large raw total in a long match may still represent a modest share of team contribution.

## Economy conversion

Gold share represents access to team resources. For damage-oriented roles, a high gold share with a much lower damage share is evidence that resources did not translate into visible damage contribution. It does not prove why this occurred.

## Survival

Deaths reduce the time available for farming, fighting, defending, and taking objectives. A review can recommend reducing high-risk engagements when deaths are high, but it must not invent the exact location or cause of each death.

## Participation

Kill participation indicates involvement in team kills. A low rate may suggest limited combat involvement, but it does not prove deliberate non-participation. A high rate supports team involvement, though it does not measure objective control or every non-kill contribution.

## Winning and losing

A win does not automatically mean the individual played well, and a loss does not automatically mean the individual played badly. Personal statistics and match result must be discussed separately unless additional evidence connects them.

## Actionable advice

Advice should name one behavior the player can attempt next match, such as maintaining safer distance, joining team fights when allies can follow, or converting a won fight into a shared objective check. Vague advice such as "play better" is not actionable.

## Evidence limits

The system must not infer exact positioning, skill accuracy, communication, target selection, intent, teammate quality, or the cause of the match result unless corresponding evidence is available. Missing values remain unknown and are never treated as zero.
