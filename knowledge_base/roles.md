# Role-based post-match interpretation

Source IDs: `official-hok-home`, `project-role-framework`

The official Honor of Kings website presents five broad play styles: Fighter, Assassin, Mage, Marksman, and Support. This project keeps Tank as a separate analytical label because the supplied match screens and evaluation cases distinguish damage-taking duties.

## Marksman

Marksmen are primarily evaluated through damage share, gold share, deaths, and participation. High raw damage alone is not enough: match duration and team damage share matter. High gold share paired with low damage share can indicate that resources were not converted into visible combat contribution. High damage-taken share and frequent deaths are warning signals for a back-line damage role, but statistics alone cannot prove the precise positioning mistake.

## Mage

Mages are evaluated through damage share, participation, deaths, and resource conversion. Low kills do not automatically indicate weak performance when assists, damage share, or control-oriented contribution are strong. Without control duration or skill-hit data, the system must not claim that control skills were used well or badly.

## Assassin

Assassins are evaluated through kills, deaths, participation, damage share, and gold conversion. Many kills combined with many deaths can reflect risky exchanges. The data can support advice to improve entry and exit discipline, but it cannot reveal the exact target chosen or movement path.

## Fighter

Fighters are evaluated through a balance of damage share, damage-taken share, participation, deaths, and gold share. The desired balance varies by hero and team composition, so the review should avoid rigid claims when hero-specific evidence is missing.

## Tank

Tanks are evaluated mainly through damage-taken share, assists, participation, and deaths. High damage taken is not automatically positive. It is stronger evidence of useful front-line contribution when participation is also high and deaths are controlled. Low kills and low damage dealt are not automatically weaknesses for this role.

## Support

Supports are evaluated mainly through assists, participation, deaths, and available protection or healing statistics. Low kills, damage, and gold are not automatically weaknesses. If healing, shielding, vision, or control data are absent, the review must explicitly avoid judging those aspects.
